## Design Context

### Users
AI builders, product teams, investors, researchers, and vendor operators who
need an objective third-party view of AI memory products and adjacent tooling.

### Use Cases
- Understand which AI memory category a system actually belongs to.
- Compare public product surfaces, evidence strength, risks, and capability gaps.
- Explore benchmark-style scorecards without assuming one internal architecture.
- Decide which systems deserve deeper diligence or re-testing.

### Brand Personality
Independent, editorial, evidence-led, and commercially aware. The interface
should feel like a public intelligence publication with a serious benchmark
explorer beneath it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Reference the structure and pacing of amplifying.ai: compact editorial header,
deep dark hero, off-white research surfaces, dense published-study cards, and a
professional benchmark section. Use original brand, copy, data, and visual
details for MemoryBench.

### Design Principles
- Public positioning first: no private architecture names or local runtime story.
- Make category boundaries explicit before showing scores.
- Separate memory APIs, temporal graphs, RAG frameworks, vector infrastructure,
  and stateful runtimes.
- Keep benchmark controls usable and dense once users enter the explorer.
- Treat scores as transparent research heuristics, not vendor endorsements.
